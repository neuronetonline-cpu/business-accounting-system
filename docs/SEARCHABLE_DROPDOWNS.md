# Searchable Dropdowns

Long-list selectors now use a searchable combobox. Type part of a product, customer, supplier, bank, or account name/code to filter the list. Use Up/Down and Enter to select.

Applied to:
- Sales/Purchases: Product and Customer/Supplier
- Customer/Supplier Credit: Name
- Banks & Cash: Bank selector, Transfer From/To Account
- Bank Reconciliation: Bank
- General Ledger manual-entry Account selector
- POS Import Center: Bank account selector

Small fixed option lists such as Payment Type remain normal dropdowns.
