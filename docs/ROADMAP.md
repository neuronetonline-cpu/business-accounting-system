# Roadmap

## Stage 1
Accounting foundation and opening balances.

## Stage 2
Daily transaction entry:
- Sales
- Customer receipts
- Purchases
- Supplier payments
- Expenses
- Bank deposits/withdrawals
- Cash transfers

## Stage 3
Operational accounting:
- Customer master
- Supplier master
- Inventory
- Credit aging
- Bank reconciliation
- General Ledger
- Trial Balance
- P&L
- Balance Sheet

## Stage 4
Management decision dashboard:
- KPIs
- Trends
- Working capital
- Stock analysis
- Alerts
- Cash-flow forecasting
